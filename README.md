### WhatsThatAbout

_What's That About?_ is a site where you can find the answers to those nagging, tip-of-the-tounge questions about show references. You know! When you're watching a movie and a sence comes up asking the question, "What was that about?" What is some arcane/out of left field/esoteric reference? A parody of a real-life person, event, place or thing? Or rumour? 

A show page is a movie, TV episode, or anime (and others soon to come), with timestamped moments explaining what the writers were referring to when adding that scene.

As an example, the Family Guy with all the cutaways scenes, Rick And Morty with references to actual science (and just as often science fiction) or The Simpsons often mentioning current events (or even predicting them).
